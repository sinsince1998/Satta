Rulebook & Table Mechanics
1. The Objective First team to hit 7 or 10 points wins the game. Scores can drop below 0 into the negatives.
2. The Auction Phase The minimum starting bid is 3. In clockwise rotation, teams either raise the bid or pass. Once you pass, you are completely out for that card. The last team standing must name their bid.
3. Option A Scoring Stakes • Success: Tap 🏆 Claim Win (+X) on that team. They receive the card value and the next card deals automatically.
• Choke / Fail: Tap -1 on the choking team. Their score drops immediately (including negatives). The card stays on screen unless you manually pass or hit Next Card.
4. Score Adjustments The -1 and +1 buttons are manual adjustments. They let you correct scoring mistakes anytime without skipping or changing the active card.
5. Dead Card Pot (+1 Bounty) If nobody is willing to open a bid of 3, click ☠️ Dead Pot (+1). The card is trashed and its point value carries over to the next card.
6. Blind Bounty Rounds (Every 5th Card) Every 5th card is cloaked behind a lock. You see the category, but the key constraint is hidden. Teams bid blind. Once bidding ends, hit 🔓 Reveal Cloaked Bounty before running the clock.
7. Sabotage Tokens Opponents can burn a Sabotage token before the clock starts. Sabotages block 2 to 3 obvious staple answers or inflict time/speaking penalties. Beating a sabotage awards an extra +1 point.
8. Peer Court vs Science Key • Bollywood, Punjabi, Pop, Music, Movies: Opponents call "Bullshit!" if they suspect a fake answer. Look up only that title. False accusations cost the challenger -1 point.
• Science & Math: Click 🔍 Reveal Science Key to view the verified answer key.
